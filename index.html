03 · The Problem

Senior living technology is 20 years behind.

You already know these. We lived every one of them as owners before we wrote a line of code.

  • Technology

    The technology gap

    Core operations still run on paper, spreadsheets, and phone tag while every other industry moved on.

  • Technology

    Vendors that don't talk

    Point solutions from disconnected vendors, with no interoperability between the systems you already pay for.

  • Technology

    High upfront and ongoing costs

    Enterprise pricing built for hospital systems, not for a community's budget.

  • Residents

    A dated resident experience

    Families arrive wearing Apple Watches and find pendants and pull cords waiting for their parents.

  • Residents

    Introducing residents to the digital age

    Seniors want to participate, but almost nothing is designed for them to actually use.

  • Operations

    The staffing burden

    Scheduling, overtime, and turnover consume leadership hours that should go to residents.

  • Operations

    Compliance and regulation

    State ratios, inspections, and license renewals tracked by memory and sticky notes.

  • Growth

    Online lead generation

    Placement agencies own your lead flow because your own website doesn't convert.

  • Growth

    Owner-operator misalignment

    Owners see the numbers, operators live the day-to-day, and the tools serve neither.

Building technology for Happy, Healthy, and Highly Affordable senior communities.

07 · Vital Connect

24x7 wellness and sleep monitoring for seniors.

Modern wearables instead of pendants and pull cords. Residents live their day; the care team sees what matters.

  • Heart rate, blood pressure, oxygen, weight, and sleep in one view
  • Residents and families check in from a simple mobile app
  • Data shareable with physicians and healthcare providers
  • A resident experience that finally matches what families expect
From a 12-month resident study
  • 55.9% of residents improved in both systolic and diastolic blood pressure
  • Stage 2 hypertension dropped from 32.8% to 19.8%
  • Residents in normal and high-normal range rose from 37.9% to 51.6%
